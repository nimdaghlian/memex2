Between 1998 and 1999, the London-based art collective BANK operated _The BANK Fax-Bak Service_. BANK members Simon Bedwell, John Russell, and Milly Thompson proof-read and copy-edited more than 300 press releases published by galleries in London and New York and faxed the faxes  back to the respective galleries.
