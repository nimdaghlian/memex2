Transcripts of episodes 1-225 of the Bu Mingbai Podcast (不明白) launched by New York Times columnist Li Yuan and Chinese journalists working for Western media organizations in May 2022. China’s harsh COVID-19 lockdowns in 2022 under that country's Zero-COVID policy and the resulting deaths and small business bankruptcies reportedly had many citizens asking, "How did my country come to this?" *Bumingbai* translates as "I have no idea."

The podcast aims to provide uncensored interviews with experts and ordinary Chinese about current affairs in China. It was blocked in China from the second episode on and the name of the podcast became a sensitive word on the Chinese Internet. People in 199 countries have listened to the podcast.
